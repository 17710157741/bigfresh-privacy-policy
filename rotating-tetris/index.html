1. Scope and game data on your device

BigFresh develops Rotating Tetris, a block puzzle game. The game does not require an account and has no in-app purchases. Your nickname, best scores, stars, unlocked themes, daily goals, milestones, preferences, the status of the one-time nickname question and selected age group are saved on your device. Your nickname is used only on an offline demo ranking board; sample opponents are fictional. We do not upload your nickname or progress to an online leaderboard or a BigFresh server. There is no separate publisher analytics or crash-reporting service.

Current boards and falling pieces are not saved. Each launch starts a new game while permanent records and preferences remain. The app disables Android app-data backup. Local game data is retained until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the game.

2. Age groups and children

A neutral age screen offers under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, and prefer not to say. Only your selected age group is saved locally; we do not ask for your birth date. In version 1.5.1, the game does not initialize advertising or advertising-consent requests for under-18 or unknown-age selections. Every selection permits the full core game. Parents or guardians can contact us about information a child may have voluntarily sent to support.

3. Advertising and third-party services

For users selecting 18 or older, the game uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertisements offering one continue per run, and frequency-limited interstitial advertisements on the completed-run score screen. The core game works offline. Ads require a network connection, and unavailable ads do not prevent a new game. No banner, app-open or third-party mediation advertising SDK is integrated in this version.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) updates applicable consent requirements and presents regional privacy forms where required. Advertising requests depend on UMP permission. If a consent update fails, ads can be requested only when UMP still reports valid permission; otherwise advertising stays unavailable. Adult advertising requests use a maximum creative content rating of G.

Google Mobile Ads may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device/account identifiers, interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostic information. These are used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The SDK encrypts transmissions using TLS. Android advertising IDs can be reset or deleted through Android settings. Google determines its retention and handling under its policies; BigFresh does not operate or directly control Google's advertising data stores. Processing may take place outside your country.

See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from partner sites and apps, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

4. Privacy choices, deletion and requests

Settings includes Privacy Choices for adult users to revisit available regional advertising choices through Google's UMP form. Its availability depends on Google's regional requirements. Future advertising requests depend on the updated permission.

Settings → Delete local data removes the saved game profile, its backup and temporary write file. Clearing app storage or uninstalling also removes local game state. These actions do not erase Google's previously held advertising information; the in-game profile deletion does not itself erase separately stored UMP choices. Use Google's privacy controls and request channels for information it processes directly. You can edit your local nickname from the rankings page.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us about information we hold. We may need proportionate verification and may retain information required to meet applicable obligations.

5. Permissions and support

The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or external-storage permissions. Network and advertising-related Android permissions support the advertising services.

If you email 17710157741@163.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these through our email provider to respond and resolve issues, and retain them only as needed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations. We do not sell support correspondence. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Contact the same address for support-data deletion requests.

1. 适用范围与本地游戏数据

3. 广告与第三方服务

对于选择 18 岁及以上的用户，游戏通过 Google AdMob 提供自愿观看的激励广告（每局最多获得一次继续机会），以及在一局完成后的成绩界面展示、受频率限制的插屏广告。核心玩法可以离线使用。广告需要联网，广告不可用不会妨碍开始新的一局。本版本未接入横幅、开屏广告或第三方聚合广告 SDK。

Google UMP 会更新适用的同意要求，并在需要时展示地区隐私表单。广告请求取决于 UMP 报告的许可状态。同意更新失败时，仅当 UMP 仍报告有效许可，才可能请求广告；否则广告保持不可用。成年用户的广告请求设置最高创意内容等级为 G。

详见 Google 隐私政策、Google 如何使用合作网站或应用中的信息及 Google Mobile Ads SDK 数据披露。
