1. Scope

This policy explains information handling for Water Sort Challenge, a casual puzzle game developed by BigFresh. It also explains how to contact us about privacy.

2. Current game features and information

At this stage, the game has no AppLovin advertising integration, account login, in-app purchases, or separate analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. BigFresh does not collect or share personal information through these features at this stage.

Any game progress or settings stored locally are held on your device rather than in a BigFresh user account. You can remove local application data using your device's application settings. This can permanently erase progress. Device or platform backup behavior is controlled by your platform settings.

3. Support correspondence

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue, and handle your request. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Support correspondence is processed through our email provider and is retained only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. We do not sell support correspondence. You can request deletion by contacting us, subject to any necessary legal retention.

4. Planned advertising

We plan to introduce AppLovin interstitial and rewarded advertisements. This is a future feature and is not a statement that the SDK currently collects data in this game. Before advertising is introduced, we will update this policy to describe the actual providers, data processing, privacy controls, and applicable audience safeguards.

AppLovin's practices are explained in its Privacy Policy. Any additional networks integrated through MAX mediation will need to be included in the updated disclosure.

5. Children and a broad audience

The game is intended as a casual puzzle experience for a broad audience. At this stage, the advertising SDK is not integrated and we do not use it to process children's information. Parents or guardians can contact us about information a child may have voluntarily sent to customer support.

For a future advertising version, the audience design and age-handling measures must be established before launch. AppLovin must not be initialized or used for users who qualify as children under applicable law. This policy does not claim that an age screen or advertising controls have already been implemented.

6. Security, requests, and third-party links

We limit use of support information to the purposes above and take reasonable care to protect it. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. For questions or requests to access, correct, or delete support information, contact 17710157741@163.com. We may ask for proportionate information to verify a request.

This policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting it involves processing by the hosting provider under GitHub's Privacy Statement. External links are subject to the respective provider's policies. This page does not embed advertising or analytics scripts.

7. Updates

We will publish changes on this page and revise the effective date. A future advertising release requires an updated policy matching that release.

1. 适用范围

本政策说明 BigFresh 开发的休闲益智游戏 Water Sort Challenge 中的信息处理方式，以及隐私问题的联系途径。

2. 当前游戏功能与信息

现阶段游戏尚未接入 AppLovin 广告，不提供账号登录、应用内购买，也未接入独立的统计或崩溃报告 SDK。BigFresh 在当前阶段不通过这些功能收集或共享个人信息。

如游戏在本地保存进度或设置，这些数据保存在你的设备上，而不是 BigFresh 用户账号中。你可以通过设备应用设置删除本地应用数据，这可能永久删除进度。设备或平台的备份行为由相应平台设置控制。

3. 客服联系信息

当你联系我们时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容，以及你主动发送的附件。这些信息用于回复、调查问题及处理请求。请勿发送密码、支付凭据或不必要的敏感信息。

客服邮件通过邮箱服务商处理，仅在解决请求和履行适用义务所需的期间保存。我们不出售客服邮件内容。你可以联系我们请求删除，但依法必须保留的信息除外。

4. 计划中的广告

我们计划接入 AppLovin 插屏广告和激励广告。这是未来功能，并不表示该 SDK 当前已在游戏中收集数据。广告启用前，我们将更新本政策，说明实际服务商、数据处理、隐私控制和适用的年龄保护措施。

AppLovin 的处理方式见其隐私政策。如果通过 MAX 聚合接入其他广告平台，更新后的政策也需要披露这些平台。

5. 儿童与广泛受众

本游戏定位为面向广泛受众的休闲益智体验。当前尚未接入广告 SDK，也不会通过该 SDK 处理儿童信息。如果儿童主动向客服发送了信息，家长或监护人可以联系我们处理。

未来广告版上线前，需要确定受众设计和年龄处理措施。对于根据适用法律属于儿童的用户，不得初始化或使用 AppLovin。本政策不表示游戏已实现年龄筛选或广告控制功能。
