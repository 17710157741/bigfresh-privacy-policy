1. Scope and local game data

BigFresh develops Water Sort Challenge, a casual puzzle game for a broad audience. The game does not require an account and has no in-app purchases. Progress, coins, earned items, scores, preferences, an age-group choice and limited gameplay diagnostic records are stored on your device. Local diagnostic records include a random installation identifier; version 1.0.0 does not upload these records to a BigFresh analytics server. We have not integrated a separate online analytics or crash-reporting service.

You can remove local game data through Android app settings or uninstall the game. This removes locally saved progress and choices. The game does not request access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

2. Age groups and children

At first launch, a neutral screen asks whether you are under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, or prefer not to say. We store only the selected group locally, not your date of birth. In version 1.0.0, users under 18 and users who do not provide an age group receive no advertisements: the game does not initialize AdMob or request advertising or advertising consent for these users. Core gameplay remains available. Parents or guardians can contact us about information a child may have sent voluntarily to support.

3. Advertising for adult users

For users selecting 18 or older, we use Google AdMob to show interstitial advertisements at suitable breaks and optional rewarded advertisements. Rewards are granted when the advertising SDK confirms completion. No additional mediation networks are integrated in this version. Before advertising is enabled, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) updates applicable privacy requirements and presents required privacy forms. If that process fails or advertising is not permitted, no advertisements are requested in that session.

Google's advertising services may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising and other device/account identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information. This processing supports advertising delivery, measurement, service performance and fraud prevention. It can occur even without a separate BigFresh analytics service. Google processes data under its Privacy Policy and describes advertising partner processing at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Retention is governed by Google's policies and applicable requirements. Advertising data is encrypted in transit.

4. Privacy controls and requests

Where required, Settings includes Privacy Choices so adult users can review or change advertising privacy choices through Google's form. Advertising is suspended while this form is opened, and future requests depend on the updated permission. This entry is shown only when Google's UMP reports it is required. Android settings also provide advertising ID controls. You can clear the game's local data to remove its saved age-group choice and see the initial screen again.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us at the email below for information we hold. For information processed directly by Google, Google's privacy controls and request channels also apply. Advertising partners may process data in countries outside your country of residence.
